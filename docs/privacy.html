Privacy Policy

Ramban English Student Manager · Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how the Ramban English Student Manager desktop application ("the App") handles information. The App is used internally by the Ramban English institute to manage student records.

1. Information the App accesses

  • Google account basics: the signed-in user's name, email address and profile picture. These are used only to show who is signed in and to record who made each change.
  • Google Drive (drive.file permission only): the App creates one folder ("Ramban English") and one Google Sheet ("Ramban English - Student Database") in the user's Google Drive, and reads and updates only those files. It cannot see, read or change any other files in the user's Drive.
  • Student records entered by institute staff: such as names, contact details, batches, attendance and fee payments.

2. Where data is stored

  • All records are stored in the institute's own Google Sheet, inside the institute's own Google account.
  • An encrypted copy is kept on the computer where the App is installed so that it can work offline. It is encrypted with Windows data protection and can only be read by that Windows user.
  • The App has no servers of its own. Data travels only between the user's computer and Google.

3. Birthday messages

If the institute turns on birthday emails, a Google Apps Script running inside the institute's own Google account emails students on their birthday using their saved email address. WhatsApp wishes are only opened in WhatsApp for staff to send manually. Nothing is sent by any third party.

4. How data is used

Data is used only to provide the App's features: managing students, batches, attendance, fees, receipts and reports for the institute. We do not sell, share, rent or transfer data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stays in the institute's Google account for as long as the institute keeps it. The institute can delete the spreadsheet from Google Drive at any time. Signing out of the App removes the stored sign-in from the computer, and uninstalling the App removes the program. Access can also be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.

7. Security

Sign-in uses Google's official OAuth process in the system browser, so the App never sees the Google password. Sign-in tokens and the offline copy are encrypted on the computer.

8. Contact

For any questions about this policy, please contact Ramban English.
