Ramban English Student Manager

Ramban English Student Manager is a private Windows desktop application used internally by the Ramban English institute to manage its students. Institute staff sign in with the institute's own Google account, and all records are saved in a Google Sheet in that account's Google Drive.

StudentsAdmissions, enrollment numbers, contact details and batches.
AttendanceBatch-wise daily attendance and monthly registers.
FeesPayments, receipts and outstanding dues.
Your own Google SheetsData stays in the institute's Google account and can be exported to Excel.

Google sign-in

The app uses Google sign-in only to identify the institute's account and to create and update its own spreadsheet in Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see any other files in the user's Drive. Read our Privacy Policy.
